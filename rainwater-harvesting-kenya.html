Overview

Plan roof catchment, first-flush control, filtration, tank capacity and overflow management. Water storage decisions should begin with actual consumption, available space, water source reliability and how long you need the stored supply to last.

Capacity and intended use

For a home, estimate daily litres used by the household and multiply by the number of reserve days. Farms, schools, apartments and businesses often need larger storage because demand is shared, peak use is higher, or supply interruptions are more costly.

Common listed Roto sizes include 1,000L, 2,000L, 3,000L, 4,000L, 5,000L, 6,000L, 8,000L, 10,000L, 16,000L, 20,000L and 24,000L. Choose the next practical size above your estimate rather than sizing exactly to average consumption.

Price, delivery and total cost

The tank price is only one part of the delivered project cost. Ask about stock, transport to your town, offloading, fittings, base preparation and any installation work. Large tanks also require access for the delivery vehicle and enough clearance to position the tank safely.

Checks before ordering

Confirm the exact product code or capacity, external dimensions, intended installation method, current manufacturer technical guidance, any warranty terms, the latest price and the delivery cost. For underground and septic products, get site-specific installation guidance before excavation.
